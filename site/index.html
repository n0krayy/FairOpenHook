The first blocks decide who eats.

At a Community Coin launch, the opening blocks are a race humans cannot win. Sniper bots watch for new pools, land in the first blocks, and take an outsized share of supply before the community even sees the token.

In the replayed SWARMLER #1212 attack, bots extracted 4.39% of supply for dust plus gas, swinging the price 22,059×. Everyone who bought after them paid the markup, and none of that value reached the project or its community.

Three mechanisms guard the opening window.

FairOpenHook lives inside the pool itself, so the rules apply to every swap, bot or human, with no frontend to bypass.

01 / DYNAMIC FEE

Snipers pay for impact

The swap fee rises with price impact, so aggressive extraction gets expensive fast while small community trades stay cheap.

0.30% base · 37.8% at 25% impact · feeK 150 · cap 90%
02 / CIRCUIT BREAKER

Extreme moves revert

Any swap that would move the price too far from the last settled price inside the opening window is reverted outright.

Reverts over 25% price moves · 100-block window
03 / WALLET CAPS

No wallet takes the launch

Opening buys are capped per wallet and rate limited, so supply spreads across the community instead of concentrating in one bot.

Max 0.5% of supply per wallet · 1 swap per 3 blocks

Captured surcharges flow to an immutable treasury, a potential new reward stream for the IMD flywheel that is independent of sell pressure.
